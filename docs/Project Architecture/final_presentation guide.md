EVALUATION CRITERIA
Technical Content – 40%
• Quality of the data science methodology
• Rigor of analysis and preprocessing
• Relevance of model selection
• Appropriateness of performance evaluation
Solution Quality – 30%
• Functionality of the application or platform
• User interface and user experience (UX)
• Integration of the ML/AI model
• Documentation and reproducibility
Presentation – 20%
• Clarity and structure of the presentation
• Effectiveness in communicating technical concepts
• Fair distribution of speaking time
• Time management
Impact and Originality – 10%
• Relevance of the addressed problem
• Innovation in the proposed approach
• Quality of insights
• Added value for end users
PRACTICAL ADVICE
TO DO
• Rehearse the presentation at least twice as a team
• Time each section
• Prepare a backup demo video
• Test the presentation on the defense computer
• Every team member must understand the entire project
• Anticipate at least five jury questions
• Favor visualizations over text
TO AVOID
• Reading slides word for word
• Text-heavy slides
• Technical jargon without explanation
• Untested or overly complex demos
• Exceeding 20 minutes
• A team member not speaking
• Excessive excuses such as “lack of time”

• Exaggerated or falsified results
